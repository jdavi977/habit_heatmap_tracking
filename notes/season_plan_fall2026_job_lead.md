## Lead goal: the 64 grid

**Center:** Signed full-time offer as `[role]` in `[field / city]` at `[salary floor]` or better by `[date]`.

Frequency tags: **D** = daily, **W** = weekly (number = times per week), **M** = monthly / one-time.

### 1. Targeting
1. Write a one-sentence target: role, industry, city, salary floor (M)
2. Build a list of ~40 companies in three tiers: dream, realistic, safe (M)
3. Read 10 job postings for the target role and list the skills that repeat (M)
4. Add 5 new companies to the list (W1)
5. Set up job alerts on 2–3 boards for the target role (M)
6. Check each tier-1 company's careers page (W1)
7. Find 3 people who have the job I want and note their path (M)
8. Review the target monthly: narrow or widen based on response rate (M)

### 2. Resume and materials
1. Master resume finished, one page (M)
2. Resume reviewed by the career centre and by one person in the field (M)
3. Tailor the top third of the resume to each application (D on application days)
4. LinkedIn updated: headline, photo, about, featured projects (M)
5. Cover letter template with 3 swappable paragraphs (M)
6. Short "about me" pitch, 30 seconds, written and said out loud (M)
7. Track which resume version gets responses (W)
8. Refresh the resume with any new project or result (M)

### 3. Skills and portfolio
1. Pick the 1–2 most-requested skills I'm weakest in from the postings (M)
2. 45 min of focused practice on those skills (W4)
3. Finish one portfolio project that matches the target role (M)
4. Write a short README / case study for each portfolio project (M)
5. Practice problems for the field (e.g. coding, case studies, technical questions) (W3)
6. Post one thing I learned or built on LinkedIn (W1)
7. Get one project reviewed by someone more experienced (M)
8. Earn one relevant certificate or course credential `[if relevant]` (M)

### 4. Networking and referrals
1. Outreach messages to alumni, recruiters, or people in target companies (W5)
2. One coffee chat or informational call (W1)
3. Attend one career fair, meetup, or info session (M2)
4. Ask professors and past supervisors for leads (M)
5. Follow up with every new contact within 48 h (D as needed)
6. Ask for a referral when a contact is at a target company with an open role (W)
7. Keep a contact log: who, when, what was said, next step (W)
8. Thank-you note after every conversation (D as needed)

### 5. Applications
1. Targeted applications (W8)
2. Apply within 3 days of a posting going up when possible (W)
3. Log every application in the tracker the same day (D on application days)
4. Follow up on applications with no response after 10 days (W1)
5. Prioritize roles with a referral or a named contact (W)
6. Weekly count: applications sent, responses, interviews (W1)
7. If response rate is under 10% after 3 weeks, change the resume or the target, not just the volume (M)
8. Apply to 2 stretch roles each week (W)

### 6. Interviews
1. Three STAR stories ready, then grow to six (M)
2. Mock interview with a friend, career centre, or AI (W2)
3. Research each company for 30 min before its interview (as needed)
4. Prepare 3 questions to ask the interviewer (as needed)
5. Record one practice answer on video and review it (W1)
6. Write a debrief after every real interview: what went well, what to fix (as needed)
7. Practice salary conversation and know my range (M)
8. Prepare a clean video-interview setup: camera, light, quiet room (M)

### 7. Mindset and energy
1. In bed by 11:00 pm (D)
2. 5 min visualization: the interview going well *and* going badly (blanking on a question, a rejection email) and my response (D)
3. Write if-then plans for obstacles (see table below) (M, review weekly)
4. Cap job work at a fixed block so it doesn't take over the whole day (D)
5. One full day off from the search (W1)
6. Read rejection emails once, log them, and move on the same day (as needed)
7. Talk to someone who got hired recently about their search (M)
8. Write one thing I'm grateful for at the end of each job block (karma/character) (D)

### 8. Offers and logistics
1. Line up 3 references and confirm they'll say yes (M)
2. Keep transcripts, ID, and work-eligibility documents ready (M)
3. Know my monthly budget and the lowest salary I can accept (M)
4. Compare offers on salary, growth, location, and team, not just pay (as needed)
5. Ask for 1 week to decide on any offer (as needed)
6. Negotiate at least once, politely (as needed)
7. Book interviews around finals and boxing sessions (W)
8. Plan the move or commute if the job is in another city (M)

## Maintenance card: boxing

**Target:** Train 3× per week and finish the season able to start a fight camp for `[next tournament]`.
- Technique + pads with coach (W1)
- Roadwork or intervals (W1)
- Light sparring at the level coach approves (W1, skip in the week before a big interview if I need to avoid a visible injury)
- Shadowbox 3 rounds (D, 10 min)
- No weight cut this season

## Minimum card: graduate

**Target:** Pass all courses and graduate on `[date]`.
- 15 min study / assignment check daily (weekdays)
- Sunday: check deadlines for the coming week
- During finals: job quota drops to 50%, boxing to 2 sessions, sleep stays fixed

## Conflicts and how to handle them

| Conflict | Plan |
|---|---|
| Finals vs. job search | Finals weeks: applications drop to 4, keep outreach and follow-ups |
| Interviews vs. sparring | No hard sparring 3 days before an interview |
| Job search taking over everything | Fixed daily block; stop at the end of the block |
| Rejections vs. motivation to train | Keep the 3 boxing sessions; they're the stress outlet |
| Career fair on a training day | Swap the session to the next free day; don't drop it |

## Obstacles: if-then plans

| Obstacle | If... | Then... |
|---|---|---|
| Rejection emails pile up | I get 3 rejections in a week | I send 2 outreach messages that same day |
| No responses | I've sent 20 applications with no interview | I get the resume reviewed again and tighten the target |
| Avoiding outreach | I'm putting off a message | I send the shortest version now, 3 sentences |
| Nerves before an interview | My heart is racing | I do my breathing routine and reread my 3 STAR stories |
| Missed a day | I break the streak | I do the next day as normal; one miss doesn't break the habit [15] |

## Daily heatmap list (10 items)

1. Job block done (applications, outreach, or project)
2. Every application logged in the tracker
3. Skill practice or practice problems
4. Follow-ups and thank-you notes sent
5. 5 min visualization (including obstacles)
6. In bed by 11:00
7. Shadowbox 3 rounds
8. Boxing session (on scheduled days)
9. 15 min school check (weekdays)
10. One-line note: what I learned or one thing I'm grateful for

## Weekly review (Sunday)

- Score each heatmap item: days done / days scheduled. **70%+ = win.**
- Pipeline numbers: applications, responses, interviews, offers.
- Send the weekly report: job search → `[friend / advisor]`, boxing → coach.
- Pick one action to add, drop, or change for next week.
