## Lead goal: the 64 grid

**Center:** Compete at `[weight class]` on `[date]` at `[tournament]`, conditioned for all 3 rounds and following my game plan.

Frequency tags: **D** = daily, **W** = weekly (number = times per week), **M** = monthly / one-time.

### 1. Conditioning
1. Roadwork 30–40 min, easy pace (W3)
2. Interval session: 6 × 3 min hard / 1 min rest, matching round timing (W2)
3. Skipping rope 3 × 3 min before every gym session (D on training days)
4. One "fight simulation": 3 × 2 min all-out on bag/pads with 1 min rest (W1)
5. Track resting heart rate each morning (D)
6. Hill or stair sprints (W1)
7. Deload week: half volume every 4th week (M)
8. Retest the fight simulation and log how I feel in round 3 (M)

### 2. Technique
1. Shadowbox 3 rounds with one focus per week (D)
2. Jab drills: 100 jabs with feet moving (W3)
3. Defense drill: slips/rolls on the slip bag (W3)
4. Footwork ladder: in/out, angles, pivot (W3)
5. Pad rounds with coach (W2)
6. Film one session and review 10 min (W1)
7. Pick 1 combo to own this month and drill it to automatic (M)
8. Ask coach for one correction after every session and write it down (D on training days)

### 3. Sparring
1. Sparring rounds at the level coach approves (W1–2)
2. Each session: one specific goal (e.g. "jab first, every exchange") (W)
3. Spar a different partner at least every other week (W)
4. Spar someone taller/longer to practise closing distance (M)
5. Write 3 lines after sparring: what worked, what didn't, one fix (W)
6. Film a sparring session and review with coach (M)
7. Work rounds under fatigue: spar after conditioning once (M)
8. Taper sparring intensity in the last 10 days before the fight (M)

### 4. Strength and mobility
1. Full-body strength, 45 min (W2)
2. Neck and core work, 10 min (W3)
3. Hip and shoulder mobility, 10 min (D)
4. Rotational power: med-ball throws (W2)
5. Grip and forearm work (W1)
6. Warm up properly before every session, no skipping (D on training days)
7. Drop heavy lifting in fight week (M)
8. Log main lifts to see trends (W)

### 5. Weight and nutrition
1. Morning weigh-in, same conditions (D)
2. Protein target: `[g]` per day (D)
3. Water target: `[L]` per day (D)
4. Weekly meal prep on Sunday (W1)
5. Walk-around weight target by each month: `[kg]` (M)
6. Plan the final week's weight approach with coach, no crash cuts (M)
7. Pre-training meal 2–3 h before (D on training days)
8. Limit alcohol and late-night eating (W review)

### 6. Sleep and recovery
1. In bed by 11:00 pm (D)
2. 7.5–9 h of sleep (D)
3. Screens off 30 min before bed (D)
4. One full rest day (W1)
5. Mobility/foam roll on rest day (W1)
6. Rate soreness 1–5 each morning; if 4–5, go light (D)
7. Report any pain to coach the same day (as needed)
8. Nap 20 min on double-session days (W)

### 7. Mental prep
1. 5 min visualization: rounds going well *and* going badly (getting hit, gassing, losing a round) and my response (D)
2. Write my fight plan in 3 lines and reread it (W1)
3. Breathing routine before sparring (D on sparring days)
4. Write if-then plans for obstacles (see table below) (M, review weekly)
5. Watch 1 amateur fight at my level and note what works (W1)
6. Rehearse walk-in, glove check, and corner instructions (M)
7. Talk to a fighter who has competed about their first fight (M)
8. Write down one thing I'm grateful for after each session (karma/character) (D)

### 8. Logistics and rules
1. Register for the tournament (M, by `[date]`)
2. Get the medical / physical done (M, by `[date]`)
3. Learn sub-novice rules: rounds, headgear, scoring (M)
4. Check gear: gloves, headgear, mouthguard, cup, boots (M)
5. Plan travel and arrival time for weigh-ins (M)
6. Confirm my corner (coach) for fight day (M)
7. Pack a fight-day bag list (M)
8. Book school and job commitments around fight week (M)

## Conflicts and how to handle them

| Conflict | Plan |
|---|---|
| Finals vs. training | Finals weeks: boxing volume drops to ~60%, sleep stays fixed |
| Interviews vs. fight week | No interviews in the final 2–3 days before the fight; offer alternate times |
| Weight cut vs. interview energy | Prepare interview answers before fight week; never cut hard |
| Visible injury before an interview | Ask for video interview; don't skip it |
| Hard sparring drains evening job block | Put job blocks on lighter days (see weekly template) |

## Obstacles: if-then plans

| Obstacle | If... | Then... |
|---|---|---|
| Too tired after class | I want to skip the gym | I go and do technique only, 30 min |
| Rejection emails pile up | I get 3 rejections in a week | I send 2 outreach messages that same day |
| Gassing in sparring | I fade in round 3 | I add a 4th interval set next week and tell coach |
| Nerves before the fight | My heart is racing on fight day | I run my breathing routine and reread my 3-line plan |
| Missed a day | I break the streak | I do the next day as normal; one miss doesn't break the habit [15] |
